WHO WE ARE

The Upskilling Labs is a citizen R&D community — not a course, not a
platform. A working lab where professionals build real skills by doing
real work with real people. Launched in January 2026 at the DC Public
Library; the format is replicable, designed so any civic community
across the US can adopt it.

The Upskilling Labs, Inc. operates as a fiscally sponsored project of
Superbloom Design, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.
Partners: Levy Strategic Design, DC Public Library, Superbloom Design.
Web: theupskillinglabs.org · Code and content built in the open
(MIT code, CC BY 4.0 content).

NAMING

The organization is "The Upskilling Labs". Shorten only to "The Labs".
NEVER abbreviate to "TUL" — not in copy, not in filenames.
Vocabulary: Build Cycle (twelve weeks, one real project), Pod,
Upskiller. Never "course", "class", "student", "lesson", or "module".

TAGLINE & KEY COPY

Tagline: "Find your people. Build your edge."
Lede: "The Labs isn't a class you sit through. It's where you practice
becoming the person you want to be — on real problems, with people who
notice."
CTA: "Join The Labs" — never "Sign up" or "Get started".
Footer brand line: "A commons for upskilling — learn by doing, in the
open. Projects, playbooks, and lessons, built like open source."
Legal line: "© 2026 The Upskilling Labs, Inc. · MIT code · CC BY 4.0
content · Built in the open."

BRAND RULES (v2 — matches the shipped app)

THEME: light-first. Warm paper #F6F4EF is the only light page
background. Dark (ink #00141B / the cover gradient) is reserved for
covers, nav bars, and footers. Never two consecutive dark sections
without a light break.

PALETTE:
  ink #00141B · navy #03232A · forest #005F68 · teal #0094A0 (accent)
  teal-deep #007882 (filled CTAs — AA on white) · red #E11D2A
  (landing CTA, alerts, the orb's red lobe)
  paper #F6F4EF · paper-edge #E8E5DF · tint #ECF3F4 · tint-edge #D8E8EA
  white #FFFFFF (cards, inputs) · charcoal #444444 (body text)
  slate #4A5557 (lede) · meta #5F6B70 (captions, labels)
  meta-soft #8A979B (placeholders, idle dots)
  on-dark text: rgba(255,255,255,.95 / .64 / .40)
  dark hairline: rgba(255,255,255,.16)
  hairlines on light: rgba(0,20,27,.10) · soft rgba(0,20,27,.07)
  ink border rgba(0,20,27,.85)
  No purples, no blues outside the teal family, no yellows.

SHAPE: ONE radius — 14px on every rectangular container (buttons,
cards, inputs, chips, tags, banners). NO pills, ever. Circles only for
genuine circles: avatars, dots, pips.

SHADOWS: rest 0 1px 2px rgba(0,20,27,.05), 0 6px 20px rgba(0,20,27,.06);
lifted 0 12px 40px rgba(0,20,27,.16). Cards: white, 1px rgba(0,20,27,.10)
border, hover translateY(-4px) + lifted shadow. Never shadows on text
or the logo.

TYPE: Geologica only — no other typeface, ever. Weights in use:
400 / 500 / 600 / 700. 4px baseline grid; tabular numerals for numbers;
no monospace. ALL TYPE IS FLUSH LEFT, ragged right — never centered,
never justified. One alignment across the whole system.
Scale (mobile → desktop ≥1024px):
  Display  44/48 -0.03em 700  →  76/80 -0.04em
  H1       32/36 -0.026em 700 →  48/52 -0.034em
  H2       26/32 -0.02em 700  →  34/40
  H3       20/28 -0.016em 600 →  24/32
  H4       17/24 -0.01em 600
  Lede     18/28 400 slate    →  20/32
  Body     16/24 400 charcoal
  Small    13/20 400 meta
  Eyebrow  12/16 +0.1em 500 UPPERCASE meta (teal-deep for emphasis) —
           plain text, NO capsule or box
  Index    18/24 700 teal-deep
  Stat     64/64 -0.04em 700 teal-deep → 88/88

BUTTONS: radius 14px, 15px/600 +0.01em, padding 15px 24px. Primary
filled = teal-deep #007882 with white text. The landing "Join The Labs"
primary is red #E11D2A. Ghost = transparent, 1px rgba(0,20,27,.85)
border. Hover: opacity .9 + rest shadow. Press: scale(0.98).
Disabled: opacity .34. Focus: 2px #0094A0 outline, offset 2px.

COVER GRADIENT (the only composed dark surface):
  radial-gradient(120% 80% at 0% 100%, rgba(0,148,160,.55), transparent 56%),
  radial-gradient(110% 70% at 100% 0%, rgba(0,148,160,.40), transparent 50%),
  linear-gradient(160deg, #00141B 0%, #03232A 55%, #005F68 120%)
  + grain noise overlay (mix-blend-mode: overlay, opacity .5).
  Teal radiates from corners and edges — never a teal blob in the center.

VOICE: warm, plain, human. Second-person "you", plain present tense,
specific nouns ("your local library", "the twelve-week Build Cycle",
"your pod") and real verbs (building, shipping, iterating). Sentence
case. No emoji, no exclamation points in marketing copy.
Words we never use: seamless, powerful, supercharge, revolutionize,
amazing, awesome, unlock, leverage, empower, game-changing,
cutting-edge, innovative, disruptive, transformative.

DESIGN LINEAGE

Our layout and typographic standards are deeply inspired by NASA's 1976
Graphics Standards Manual (Danne & Blackburn) and the International
Typographic Style behind it: a strict grid, a systematic type scale, a
restrained palette with a single red accent, and type set flush left,
ragged right — never centered. Expressive round mark, disciplined
flush-left system.

LOGO

Primary lockup: the orb mark (teal top, red lower lobe, teal swoosh)
plus the sentence-case wordmark "The Upskilling Labs" in white.
The orb is a direct homage to the NASA "meatball" insignia — a round
emblem with a vector swoosh cutting across it.
The lockup is a white knockout — place it on dark ink or cover surfaces
only, with clear space. In the app it sits in the ink nav bar
(76px bar, 56px logo) and the footer. On paper/light surfaces use the
orb-on-light mark instead. Partner marks (DC Public Library, Levy
Strategic Design, Superbloom Design) are white knockouts on the footer
rail only — never in the hero. No drop shadows on the mark.

PHOTOGRAPHY

Used rarely — external marketing only. The photo reads as texture,
never as subject. Grayscale the photo, then apply the cover tint
(the cover gradient at rgba .90–.93 opacities) and a scrim so text
stays legible. Good subjects: people working candidly, ambient or
underexposed cool light, library and civic contexts, workshop moments.
Never: horizon lines or perspective grids, stairs, blinds, railings or
striped surfaces, posed stock, warm or golden-hour tones.

LOGO CLEAR SPACE & MINIMUM SIZE

Keep clear space equal to the orb's height on all four sides — no text,
marks, or edges inside it. Minimum size: screen never below 24px tall
(the app nav lockup is 56px); print never below 0.5" / 13 mm wide.
Below that, use the orb mark alone.

PRINT COLOR REPRODUCTION (CMYK)

For anything printed. CMYK is a reference conversion from the sRGB hex —
on press, confirm against a printed proof and match critical color to
the HEX or Pantone. Pantone values are nearest approximate. No pastels.

ACCESSIBILITY

Meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large). On paper/white, body text
uses ink, charcoal, slate, or meta (all pass); meta-soft #8A979B is
decorative only (2.7:1) — never body text. Teal #0094A0 is an accent
(3.7:1 on white — large only); use teal-deep #007882 for text and links
on light (5.2:1). On ink, white and teal both pass. Visible focus on
every interactive element: 2px #0094A0 outline, 2px offset. Inputs 16px
minimum (prevents iOS zoom). Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Always
write alt text; the logo's alt is "The Upskilling Labs".

GLOSSARY

The Labs — the only shortening of The Upskilling Labs. Never "TUL".
Build Cycle — the twelve-week program; one real project, W0 to W12.
Pod — a small cross-functional team (5–8) formed early in a cycle.
Project Team — the 3–5 who carry a project after Meet the Projects (W8).
Upskiller — a participant (the copy word; "Participant" in code).
Poderator — a pod's shepherd/facilitator ("shepherd" in the UI).
Learning Log — the weekly practice ritual (formerly the "pulse check").
Demo Day — the W12 showcase where Project Teams present prototypes.
Not an LMS — never "course," "class," "student," "lesson," or "module".
